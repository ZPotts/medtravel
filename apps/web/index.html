A 4.9 rating doesn’t tell you the surgery was good.

Friendly staff and a nice waiting room earn stars. Skilled, safe treatment is harder to see. So we show what travelers say and what we can verify as two separate things.

What travelers say

Ratings and review counts from public sources. A perfect score from nine people is pulled toward the average, so it can’t beat a 4.7 from a thousand.

What we can verify

Five checks: a named, registered dentist, facility accreditation, professional membership, documented materials, and a written warranty. “Not verified yet” means we haven’t confirmed it, not that it failed.

How easy it is to be there

Every city and neighborhood gets a score out of 5 for where you’ll stay, how much English is spoken, and nearby resources like pharmacies and hospitals. We show the US State Department travel advisory next to every city.

Before you book anything. The CDC has guidance for people who travel abroad for care, including the risks and what to ask a clinic. Read the CDC guidance on medical tourism.
