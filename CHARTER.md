# MEDITRAVEL

## Who we serve
People considering treatment abroad for better access, quality, or value
than the US offers, and who want to decide with confidence, not a sales pitch.

## The problem we solve
Quality care abroad is hard to find and compare, and most information
comes from parties paid to sell it. MediTravel is the starting point: tell
us what you need, and we return a verified, comparable list of clinics
that fit.
